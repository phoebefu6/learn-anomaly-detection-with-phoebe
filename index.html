easy - everyone medium hands-on / deciding hardest
An honest note on detection: anomalies are rare, so accuracy lies - a detector that flags nothing is 99% accurate and useless. This course measures with precision and recall from the first session, treats alert fatigue as the real failure mode, and teaches the published canon - the Chandola survey, LOF, Isolation Forest, and One-Class SVM - honestly. Most real detection is unsupervised; labels are scarce and late. Deep methods are surveyed and cited, not trained from scratch. The live detector is real math on a small labelled teaching series, not a production system.
